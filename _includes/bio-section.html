About Me

I am a Software Developer at IBM Research Zürich and recently completed my MSc in Computer Science at ETH Zürich.

My work sits at the intersection of data systems, ML systems, and applied machine learning, with recent research on provisioning-aware query routing for heterogeneous lakehouse engines. My ETH thesis received a 6/6; a related workshop paper was accepted at SIGMOD, and an extended version is under review at a database systems venue.

Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at AB InBev, building forecasting, recommender, and optimization systems for supply-chain and commercial analytics.

I graduated Department Rank 1 and Gold Medalist from IIT Bombay, with a parallel background in journalism and technical communication.
